> **"Do not collect resources. Master one resource per subject."**

### 1. Section 1: Engineering Mathematics & Discrete Mathematics

#### A. Discrete Mathematics
*(Propositional and first-order logic; Sets, relations, functions, partial orders and lattices; Monoids, Groups; Graphs: connectivity, matching, colouring; Combinatorics: counting, recurrence relations, generating functions)*

- **Primary Learning Resource [FREE]**:
  - **Platform/Channel**: [GO Classes for GATE CS (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@GOClassesforGATECS) & [GO Classes Free Course Platform](https://www.goclasses.in/)
  - **Course / Playlist**: *Discrete Mathematics for GATE CS by Deepak Poonia Sir & Sachin Mittal Sir*
  - **Coverage**: 100% of IIT Madras GATE CS syllabus. Unmatched rigor on First Order Logic, Group Theory, Graph Colouring/Matching, and Generating Functions.
  - **How to use**: Watch every lecture sequentially. Solve the class homework questions in their lecture notes.
- **Backup Resource [FREE]**:
  - **Platform/Channel**: [Amit Khurana (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@AmitKhuranaSir) — *Discrete Mathematics Playlist* (Great for step-by-step problem-solving).
- **Notes / Short Notes**: Self-made lecture summaries + GO Classes lecture PDF slides.
- **PYQ Source [FREE]**: [GATE Overflow Discrete Mathematics Category](https://gateoverflow.in/questions/discrete-mathematics).
- **Standard Reference Book**: *Discrete Mathematics and its Applications* by Kenneth H. Rosen (7th/8th Edition) — Chapter 1, 2, 6, 8, 9, 10 for reference exercises.

#### B. Linear Algebra
*(Matrices, determinants, system of linear equations, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, LU decomposition)*

- **Primary Learning Resource [FREE]**:

- **Backup Resource [FREE]**:
  - **Platform/Channel**: [Gajendra Purohit (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@GajendraPurohit) — Linear Algebra playlist (for mechanical shortcut tricks).
- **PYQ Source [FREE]**: [GATE Overflow Linear Algebra](https://gateoverflow.in/questions/linear-algebra).
- **Standard Reference Book**: *Introduction to Linear Algebra* by Gilbert Strang.
